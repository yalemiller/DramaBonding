About the show

Friends who should know better, rolling dice in the dark and calling it theatre.

Drama Bonding is an actual-play tabletop podcast, told in books. Each book is a complete tale — begin anywhere, though the wood remembers if you skip ahead.

New chapters arrive every other Wednesday. Expect heartbreak, poor decisions, and at least one song per session.
